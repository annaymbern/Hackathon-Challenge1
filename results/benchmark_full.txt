How much the finetuned model moves between runs
===============================================
Training is random, so we trained it twice before fixing the seed:

                          run 1   run 2
precision@3                87.4    88.6
prec. other language       84.6    86.2
drinkable                  64.3    41.2
fruit                      77.6    86.4
greek                      98.0    98.0
plain                      92.5    96.2

The headline is stable: both runs beat Jaccard by 13 to 14 points and both
clear the rule from Work 3. The per-type numbers are not stable, because each
type has only one to seventeen test queries and two suggestions landing
differently move a percentage by twenty points.

We fixed the seed so that a rerun reproduces a number, and we report the
per-type figures as a direction, not as measurements. Only the overall figure
and the language split rest on enough cases to be worth quoting.

The methods that need no model are deterministic and identical in both runs.
