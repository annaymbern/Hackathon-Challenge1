# Hackathon Challenge – Group 1

PAIBS project (ESADE) by Patricia Cerdà, Helena Collart, Jana Biosca and Anna Ymbern.

We're working with Open Food Facts data on two ideas:
- **Idea 3 – Private-label dupe finder:** finds the own-brand version of a branded product and compares price and environmental impact.
- **Idea 4 – Best quality per € finder:** ranks products by how healthy and sustainable they are for their price.

## Folders
- `data/` – our own small lists (own brands, known dupes)
- `notebooks/` – Colab notebooks
- `src/` – our code: cleaning the data and the models
- `app/` – the Streamlit demo

Our decisions and changes are in [LOG.md](LOG.md).
